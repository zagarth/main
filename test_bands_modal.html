Enhanced Bands Modal - Adaptive Series Display

Products WITH Series Data

These products have series information in the database and will show width alternatives and series siblings.

Products WITHOUT Series Data

These products lack series info but may show pattern siblings (T006 matches).

Expected Behavior

  • T18 Series Products: Should show "Available Widths in Tiffany Comfort Curve Series" with 3mm, 4mm, 5mm, 6mm, 7mm, 8mm options
  • T006 Products: Should show "Related Products" with T006 pattern siblings (1T006L, 1T006M)
  • Isolated Products: Should show no related products section (adaptive)
  • Current Product: Should be highlighted with dark background in related products
  • Clickable Siblings: Should open their modal when clicked
